The Teacher's Journey

STEP 01

Secure Login

Start your session through our secure authentication portal. Your data is protected with encryption and seamlessly synchronized across your devices.

STEP 02

Interactive Dashboard

Manage all your subjects, track attendance records, organize student rosters, and monitor classroom activities from one beautiful, unified dashboard.

STEP 03

Course Management

Add a subject in seconds and let ClickClass handle the setup, student organization, and attendance tracking.

STEP 04

FaceID Attendance

Use advanced AI-powered facial recognition to simplify classroom attendance. Capture a single classroom photo, and ClickClass automatically detects and recognizes enrolled students, helping you mark attendance quickly and accurately.

STEP 05

Voice ID Attendance

Take attendance hands-free with AI-powered voice recognition. Students speak their names one by one, and the system identifies enrolled students using voice matching to automatically record attendance.

STEP 06

Actionable Records

Review and manage historical attendance records with ease. Monitor AI confidence scores, download detailed CSV reports, and track long-term student attendance to make informed decisions.
